# Manifesto

This is not a theory about what AI and humans should do together.

It is a method extracted from what we discovered we had to do when attempting serious work.

The method is not finished. It cannot be finished in isolation. It becomes real only when applied to problems that matter enough to reveal its weaknesses.

Each time the system fails to preserve what matters, we encode the remedy back into the system itself.

The repository is therefore simultaneously:
- a description of how to think
- a working environment for that thinking
- an archive of what the thinking produced
- a historical record of what we learned we needed to change

This is how philosophy becomes executable.
